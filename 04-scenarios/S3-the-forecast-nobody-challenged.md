# S3 - The forecast nobody challenged

> Fictional, illustrative scenario. Numbers are invented.

**Stage:** PLAN  |  **Drivers:** anchoring, optimism, groupthink, authority

## Setup
The S&OP team adopts the commercial director's demand number every month, overriding the statistical model. Minutes for nine months show no alternatives discussed. Inventory of a slow product builds; a key supplier is asked to reserve capacity on a take-or-pay basis.

## Behavioral drivers
- **Anchoring** on last year's growth.
- **Optimism** about a new customer win.
- **Groupthink and authority:** the senior number becomes the consensus.

## Hard-data signals
- Forecast bias consistently positive (plan above actual) for nine months.
- Days of inventory rising; take-or-pay exposure growing in the contract.

## Qualitative signals
- Q6 (no dissent in minutes), Q8 (overrides by one role), Q11.

## What AI surfaces
- **AI-1** forecast-override log: overrides by the same senior role, always upward.
- **AI-3** aggregate minutes review: no alternatives or risks recorded in 9 of 9 meetings; deferral wording with no owner.
- **AI-5** pairing: positive bias + rising inventory + take-or-pay exposure.
- Limitation: minutes may not capture discussion; AI cannot know what was said off-record.

## Analyst judgment
Was the override based on real information the model lacked? The analyst asks for the documented basis. The finding, if any, is about the S&OP process lacking a challenge step, not about any attendee.

## Human handoff
To the **S&OP process owner** and **CFO** (risk appetite on inventory and take-or-pay). See [handoff H3](../06-human-in-the-loop/README.md#h3---forecast-overrides-and-no-recorded-dissent).

## Audit angle
- **Procedure:** Inspect minutes and override log; observe one S&OP meeting (with notice); walkthrough of how the consensus number is set; analytics on forecast bias.
- **Evidence:** Override log, minutes, forecast-accuracy data, contract terms. Observation notes documented and corroborated with the override log.

## Controls and response
- Overrides above a threshold need a written basis and are tracked for accuracy.
- Named "challenge" role in S&OP; minutes record alternatives considered.
- Take-or-pay commitments above a limit need CFO approval.
